# Terms

A summary, not legal advice: read Norges Bank's own words before relying on
them. Terms change; these were read on 19 November 2026.

From Norges Bank's disclaimer
([norges-bank.no/en/disclaimer](https://www.norges-bank.no/en/disclaimer/))
and its NOWA FAQ
([norges-bank.no](https://www.norges-bank.no/en/topics/liquidity-and-markets/nowa/faq-nowa/)):

- **Copies.** Allowed with Norges Bank quoted as the source and no change to
  the content.
- **Linking.** No inline linking.
- **Liability.** Norges Bank accepts none.
- **NOWA.** Published free; it needs no licence.
- **Commercial use.** Not addressed.
- **Limits.** No rate limit is stated.

Each dataset describes these terms in words, shown on the Data sources
page, and declares no licence. The licence a deployment admin sets there is
the one enforced; the plugin says nothing of whether your deployment meets
anyone's terms.

This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Norges Bank.
